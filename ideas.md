# Chi-Zaram Website Design Brainstorm

## Chosen Direction: Harvest Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial commerce, drawing from premium food magazines, modern African packaging, and tactile still-life photography rather than generic e-commerce grids.

### Core Principles
1. **Nature with structure:** Use organic curves, botanical details, and grain textures inside a disciplined editorial frame.
2. **Warm authority:** Let rich red and charcoal signal confidence while cream and muted green keep the experience grounded and approachable.
3. **Product-first storytelling:** Every visual and section should move the visitor from curiosity to understanding to WhatsApp enquiry.
4. **Asymmetrical rhythm:** Prefer offset columns, anchored callouts, and varied section heights over repetitive centered blocks.

### Color Philosophy
The signature color is **Palm Crimson** (#9f2f2a), inspired by the visual language already present in the supplied brand materials and the depth of red palm oil. It is paired with toasted gold (#c99a4a) for warmth and value, palm leaf green (#435847) for provenance, and oat cream (#f4efe6) for breathing room. The palette should feel natural and food-safe, not overly rustic: dark charcoal text carries clarity, while small crimson moments create recognition and momentum.

### Layout Paradigm
A scrolling editorial canvas with a slim utility bar, a split hero, floating product notes, and staggered content bands. Use one-column reading widths for story sections, but allow imagery and product cards to break the frame at the edges. Calls to action should appear as anchored moments in the narrative instead of identical buttons repeated in every section.

### Signature Elements
- A cropped botanical/palm-leaf line illustration used as a quiet frame around key sections.
- Small uppercase editorial labels with red rules, resembling a product journal or wholesale ledger.
- Rounded rectangular product stamps in crimson and gold, used sparingly for pack size, category, and availability.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a well-made product: responsive, direct, and tactile. Buttons compress subtly on press; cards lift by a few pixels on hover; WhatsApp CTAs are always obvious without becoming noisy. Mobile navigation should open as a clean sheet, and enquiry actions should preserve the visitor's context.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for controls and cards. Fade and translate hero content upward in small staggered groups. Let the botanical accent drift by a few pixels only where reduced motion is not requested. Avoid looping motion that competes with product content. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing non-essential transforms and entrance animation.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for high-impact headlines and **Manrope** for navigation, body copy, labels, and buttons. Headlines should be large and slightly tight, with italic emphasis for one phrase in major statements. Labels use 11px–12px uppercase text with generous tracking. Body copy should remain at 16px–18px with a comfortable 1.6 line-height.

### Brand Essence
CHI-ZARAM is the Nigerian everyday-products brand for households, resellers, and growing businesses who want quality, value, and a direct path to order. Personality: **warm, assured, resourceful**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound declarative and sensory. CTAs are specific and action-led. Microcopy explains what happens next and avoids overclaiming unverified product or regulatory details.

Example lines:
- “Bring home the taste of something well made.”
- “Tell us what you need. We’ll confirm the right supply.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a bold symbol-first mark: a simplified palm fruit cluster enclosed by a hand-drawn oval, with one leaf cutting through the top edge. The mark should stand on its own in the header and favicon; pair it with a custom stacked wordmark lockup in the site header rather than relying on a default text logo.

### Signature Brand Color
**Palm Crimson — #9f2f2a**.

### Content Commitments
- Present CHI-ZARAM Palm Oil as the flagship product with its documented 5-litre hero pack.
- Organize the broader portfolio into Palm Oil, Cleaning Essentials, Fabrics Collections, Oil Perfume, and Retail & Bulk Supply.
- Keep current prices, addresses, regulatory numbers, and unverified claims out of permanent copy; direct visitors to WhatsApp for live availability and fulfilment details.
- Use WhatsApp-first conversion with a prefilled enquiry message.

### Style Decisions
- Keep the page light and editorial; do not use purple gradients, neon accents, or generic SaaS patterns.
- Use imagery as an editorial crop with text-safe areas, never as a full-bleed background without a contrast overlay.
- Treat every section as a part of one continuous harvest-to-home story.
